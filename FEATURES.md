Feature Updates: POS System
Feature 1: Vendor Management
•	Description:
A vendor management feature allows the POS system to store and manage information about suppliers/vendors. For example, Square allows businesses to create, edit, import, filter, and deactivate vendors and associate vendors with specific products.
•	Key Functionality:
o	Create and maintain vendor profiles.
o	Store vendor information and vendor codes.
o	Link products to the vendors that supply them.
o	Search and filter products by vendor.
o	Update vendor-specific product costs.
o	Deactivate vendors that are no longer being used.
o	Relevance to the system: This helps the POS system keep organized supplier records and makes it easier to know which vendor supplies each product.
Feature 2: Purchase Order and Inventory Management
•	Description:
A purchase-order feature allows the business to order products from vendors and track the products when they are received. Square supports creating purchase orders, selecting vendors and products, recording quantities and unit costs, and receiving inventory.
•	Key Functionality:
o	Create purchase orders for vendors.
o	Select products from the inventory list.
o	Record quantities, unit costs, and expected delivery dates.
o	Send purchase orders to vendors.
o	Record fully or partially received items.
o	Update inventory when stock is received.
o	Track purchase-order status and history.
o	Relevance to the system: This connects vendor relations with inventory control, helping the business know what has been ordered, what has arrived, and what stock is available.
